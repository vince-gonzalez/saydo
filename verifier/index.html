SayDo receipt verifier

A SayDo receipt is a hash-chained record of a conformance run. This page recomputes the whole chain from its genesis anchor, entirely in your browser — no request leaves this page, no account, no trust in the issuer. Change one byte of a receipt and the walk fails at that row. The chain rule is row_hash = sha256(prev_hash + canonical_row).

The receipt records what a monitor observed during the run. That monitor watches the Python runtime, not the kernel: it catches drift, accidents, and false declarations made by ordinary code, and it is not a sandbox. Read a green result as “this run's observations stayed inside the declared envelope,” not as a proof of safety. · F-Keys
